# MiBolt cart, context and receipt story test plan

## Application Overview

MiBolt is a Hungarian demo storefront: an Angular application served from the Playwright base URL with the in-page MSW mock behind it (no backend, no database). These five scenarios come from `docs/spec/test-scenarios.md` and cover screens the shop already has: the product page `/telefonok/<slug>`, the cart `/penztar/kosar`, the checkout wizard under `/penztar/` and the receipt `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`. Every scenario starts in a fresh browser context on the in-page MSW mock, which starts signed in and keeps its state (cart, checkout draft, payment intents, receipts) in that context's `sessionStorage` under `mi-bolt.snapshot.<scenario>`; one fresh context IS an empty cart with no sign-in step, which is why the five scenarios are independent and can run in any order.

Products are never named by slug or product name: every scenario picks its phone by what the screen shows and reads the slug from the address bar only when a later step needs it. Prices come from the `app-price-block` primitive, whose digits sit in `price-amount` with the `Ft` glyph as a separate span beside them. Controls built on the `app-button` primitive are host elements carrying the test id, so a press targets the native `button` inside them: `page.getByTestId('<id>').getByRole('button')`. The product page mounts its purchase call to action twice: the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` is the one at 1024 px and wider, and the sticky rail's `purchase-cta` is the one below 1024 px, where the desktop control is in the DOM but not visible. Every step below is written for both widths and names both mounts where they differ.

The cash-on-delivery walk of `specs/storefront-integration.md` scenario 3.7 is reused to reach a receipt and is not repeated here as a scenario: pick a card whose `price-amount` is at most 300 000 Ft (the mock's cash-on-delivery ceiling), add it, run the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567", choose "Házhoz szállítás" with "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1." and "Utánvét", tick `review-consent-terms-input` and press the inner button of `review-submit`.

One row is planned against the nearest behaviour the screen actually offers rather than a control it does not have. E104: "Inspect header and start new cart" → "Badge reflects empty/new cart, not committed items." The receipt page renders under the checkout layout, whose header (`checkout-header`) carries the brand, the "Demó pénztár" label and `back-to-shop` but no `cart-badge` at all, so the badge cannot be inspected on the receipt screen itself. Scenario 1.5 therefore inspects the shop header's `cart-badge` on the screen `success-new-shopping` lands on, and covers every part of the row that this screen does offer.

Two facts govern scenario 1.1's second browser context. `browser.newContext()` is the raw API and takes only the options it is handed: the test runner applies the config's `use` (base URL, viewport) to the `context`/`page` fixtures, not to a context a test builds itself, and the API gives a context viewport a 1280x720 default when none is passed. Checked in this planning browser, a bare `newContext()` did come back carrying the base URL and the 1920x1080 viewport, but that is this planning harness wrapping the browser, not a spec run; the test therefore passes `baseURL` and `viewport` explicitly (`testInfo.project.use.baseURL` and `testInfo.project.use.viewport`) and closes the context at the end.

Two numbers are read off the screens here rather than invented: the mock's cash-on-delivery ceiling of 300 000 Ft (`CASH_ON_DELIVERY_MAX_CART_HUF` in `frontend/projects/shop-mocks/src/checkout/checkout-store.ts`) and the price-ascending catalogue whose cheapest in-stock cards start at "69 990" Ft and "89 990" Ft, both below 150 000 Ft.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Shop: cart, context and receipt stories

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. [E011] two browser contexts keep their own carts

**File:** `e2e/integration/cart-story-two-contexts-isolation.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. In the test's own page open `/telefonok?sort=price_asc` and wait for the grid (at least one `product-card`). Open the first `product-card` that has no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, note the name in `product-name` and the digits of its "Készülékár" `price-amount` (call this phone A), and press the add control of the mount the viewport shows: the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, `purchase-cta` below 1024 px. - expect: `product-stock` reads "Raktáron a demó készletben" and the "Készülékár" amount is at most 300 000 Ft, so the later cash-on-delivery step stays available. - expect: The confirmation reads "<A name>, <colour>, <n> GB – 1 darab a kosárban." and `cart-badge` in the shop header reads "1".
2. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-page` is visible with exactly one `cart-line`: its `cart-line-name` is A's name and its `cart-line-quantity` reads "1 db". - expect: `cart-count` reads "Kosárban található termékek (1)" and `cart-continue` ("Tovább az adatokhoz") is enabled. - expect: This page's header is the checkout header, so no `cart-badge` is rendered here — the badge is read on a shop screen.
3. Open a second browser context with `browser.newContext({ baseURL: testInfo.project.use.baseURL, viewport: testInfo.project.use.viewport })`, open a page in it, and open `/telefonok?sort=price_asc&mi-scenario=fail-order-read` in that page. - expect: `catalog-grid` is rendered and the first cards' `price-amount` ascend from "69 990" Ft, so the base URL and the fault query both reached the second page. - expect: `cart-badge` reads "0": the second context starts with a cart of its own, empty, and the demo gate is signed in without a sign-in step.
4. In the second page open the first `product-card` that has no `product-card-stock` marker, whose name differs from A, and whose `price-amount` is at most 150 000 Ft (so that two of them stay under the 300 000 Ft cash-on-delivery ceiling); note its name (call it B), and press the add control of the mount the viewport shows twice, without changing the selected colour or storage. - expect: The confirmation after the second press reads "<B name>, <colour>, <n> GB – 2 darab a kosárban." - expect: This page's `cart-badge` reads "2", while the first page's `cart-badge` (read on its shop screen) still reads "1": neither header counts the other context's cart.
5. Open the cart in each page with its own header's `cart-link` and compare the two carts. - expect: Each `cart-page` holds exactly one `cart-line`: the first page's line is A with `cart-line-quantity` "1 db", the second page's line is B with `cart-line-quantity` "2 db". - expect: Neither cart lists the other context's phone: the two `cart-line-name` values differ, and each page's `cart-count` reads "Kosárban található termékek (1)". - expect: Each page's "Részösszeg" `price-amount` equals its own line's price only, so no line of the other cart reached it.
6. Read each page's `sessionStorage` keys with a page script. - expect: The first page holds `mi-bolt.snapshot.default` and the second holds `mi-bolt.snapshot.fail-order-read`. - expect: Neither page holds the other's snapshot key, so no cart, receipt or storage key leaks across the two contexts.
7. Walk both carts to a receipt with the cash-on-delivery walk of `specs/storefront-integration.md` scenario 3.7 (contact "Teszt Elek" / "teszt@example.test" / "+36 30 123 4567", delivery "Házhoz szállítás" with "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", payment "Utánvét", terms tick, `review-submit`), first in the first page, then in the second page — every step of each walk is in-app navigation, which keeps the second page's fault scenario, so no `page.goto` is needed until the receipt is reopened. - expect: Each page lands on its own `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` with an order id in the path. - expect: The first page shows `order-receipt` and `success-heading` reads "Elkészült a demórendelésed.", with exactly one `receipt-line` naming A and `receipt-row-payment` containing "Utánvét". - expect: The second page shows its own `order-receipt`, a non-empty `receipt-order-number` and `receipt-row-payment` containing "Utánvét", so the fault context reaches a receipt of its own.
8. Reopen the second page's receipt as a fresh page load inside the fault: navigate that page to `/penztar/siker/<orderId>?mi-scenario=fail-order-read`, with the order id read from its address bar. - expect: `success-heading` reads "A betöltés nem sikerült" and `success-error` is visible with the title "A visszatöltés nem sikerült", the message "A rendelés visszatöltése sikertelen." and the single control "Visszatöltés újra". - expect: `order-receipt`, `receipt-order-number` and `success-actions` are not rendered on that page, so the fault context cannot read its receipt back. - expect: The first page's receipt is untouched: `order-receipt` is still visible with the same `receipt-order-number` — the fault in one context never reaches the other.
9. Close the second context with `context.close()`. - expect: The second page is gone, the test's own page still renders its `order-receipt` with the same `receipt-order-number`, and no cart, receipt or storage key crossed between the two contexts at any point.

#### 1.2. [E054] two variants of one phone become two cart lines

**File:** `e2e/integration/cart-story-two-variant-lines.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid (at least one `product-card`). Open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker whose `product-card-swatches` group holds two or more `product-card-swatch` buttons, through its `product-card-cta`. - expect: `product-page` is visible, `product-name` is not empty and the URL carries that card's `?variant=<id>`. - expect: Either `variant-color-group` lists more than one `variant-color-*` button or `variant-storage-group` lists more than one `variant-storage-*` button — whichever group offers more than one option is the one the two variants are taken from.
2. Read the product page's first variant and add it: note the "Készülékár" `price-amount` (call it P1) and the selected colour and storage, then press the add control of the mount the viewport shows (the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, `purchase-cta` below 1024 px). - expect: `product-stock` reads "Raktáron a demó készletben" and the add control is enabled. - expect: The confirmation reads "<product name>, <colour>, <n> GB – 1 darab a kosárban." and `cart-badge` reads "1".
3. Choose a second variant of the same phone: press a different-colour `variant-color-*` button whose `aria-pressed` is "false", or a different-storage `variant-storage-*` button whose `aria-pressed` is "false" — one that is not disabled. If `product-stock` then reads "Ez a konfiguráció elfogyott a demó készletből.", choose another such option until an in-stock one is selected. - expect: The chosen button carries `aria-pressed="true"` and its group's legend names the new choice ("Szín: <name>" or "Tárhely: <n> GB"). - expect: `product-stock` reads "Raktáron a demó készletben" and the "Készülékár" `price-amount` (call it P2) is readable.
4. Press the add control of the mount the viewport shows for this second variant, without touching the selection afterwards. - expect: The confirmation reads "<product name>, <colour>, <n> GB – 1 darab a kosárban." and `cart-badge` reads "2".
5. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-page` is visible with exactly two `cart-line` elements, both `cart-line-name` elements reading the phone's name. - expect: Their variant lines differ: one reads "<first colour> · <first storage> GB" and the other "<second colour> · <second storage> GB", so the two variants are two separate lines rather than one merged line. - expect: Each line's `cart-line-quantity` reads "1 db", and their "Részösszeg" `price-amount` read P1 and P2 — the two unit prices read from the lines. - expect: `cart-count` reads "Kosárban található termékek (2)" and `cart-continue` is enabled.
6. Read the cart's totals. - expect: `order-summary-subtotal`'s `price-amount` reads exactly P1 + P2, the sum of the two unit prices read from the cart lines. - expect: `order-summary-total` still shows the "Végösszeg" placeholder ("—" with "Nincs megjeleníthető ár."), because the shipping fee is chosen on the delivery step. - expect: No line is duplicated, merged or re-priced: the two `cart-line` elements and their `price-amount` values are the two variants added above.

#### 1.3. [E056] removing one line keeps the other

**File:** `e2e/integration/cart-story-remove-one-line.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok?sort=price_asc` and wait for the grid. Open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, press the add control of the mount the viewport shows (the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, `purchase-cta` below 1024 px), note its name (call it A) and its "Készülékár" `price-amount` (PA), then go back to `/telefonok?sort=price_asc`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker whose name differs from A, add it the same way, and note its name (call it B) and its price (PB). - expect: Each confirmation names its own product, colour and storage and reads "1 darab a kosárban." - expect: `cart-badge` reads "2" in the shop header after the second add.
2. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-page` is visible with exactly two `cart-line` elements, their `cart-line-name` reading A and B in the order they were added. - expect: Each line's `cart-line-quantity` reads "1 db" and each line's "Részösszeg" `price-amount` reads PA and PB respectively. - expect: `cart-count` reads "Kosárban található termékek (2)" and `order-summary-subtotal`'s `price-amount` reads PA + PB. - expect: `cart-line-remove` is rendered once per line, its text "Törlöm" and its accessible name "Törlöm: <product name>".
3. Press the `cart-line-remove` of the line whose `cart-line-name` is B. - expect: Exactly one `cart-line` remains and its `cart-line-name` is A: only the selected line was removed. - expect: The remaining line keeps its own selection untouched: `cart-line-name` A, `cart-line-quantity` "1 db" and "Részösszeg" `price-amount` PA. - expect: `cart-count` reads "Kosárban található termékek (1)" and `order-summary-subtotal`'s `price-amount` reads PA, so the totals follow the removal. - expect: No `cart-line` on the page carries B's name, so the removed selection is not offered again, and `order-summary-total` still shows the "Végösszeg" placeholder ("—" with "Nincs megjeleníthető ár.").
4. Read the header badge, which the cart screen itself does not carry: press the checkout header's `back-to-shop` ("Vissza a shopba"). - expect: The browser is on `/telefonok` with the `catalog-grid` rendered and the shop header's `cart-badge` reads "1". - expect: `cart-link`'s accessible name reads "Kosár megnyitása, 1 termék", so the badge and the cart count both follow the single remaining line.

#### 1.4. [E069] new shopping after a completed receipt

**File:** `e2e/integration/cart-story-new-shopping-after-receipt.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Reach a receipt with the cash-on-delivery walk of `specs/storefront-integration.md` scenario 3.7: open `/telefonok?sort=price_asc`, pick the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker whose `price-amount` is at most 300 000 Ft, open it through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the add control of the mount the viewport shows, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`, then on the delivery step choose "Házhoz szállítás", fill "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", choose "Utánvét", press `delivery-confirm`, tick `review-consent-terms-input` and press the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` with an order id in the path, `order-receipt` is visible and `success-heading` reads "Elkészült a demórendelésed.". - expect: `receipt-lines` lists exactly one `receipt-line` with "1 db" and `receipt-row-status` reads "Demó rendelés visszaigazolva". - expect: `success-actions` offers `success-new-shopping` ("Vissza a telefonokhoz") and the hint "Az új vásárlás friss kosarat hoz létre; a korábbi rendelés adatai továbbra is elérhetők ezen az URL-en." - expect: Note the order id the address bar carries, for the later Back step.
2. Press the inner button of `success-new-shopping` ("Vissza a telefonokhoz"), then press the browser's Back once and its Forward once. - expect: `success-new-shopping` lands the browser on `/telefonok` with the `catalog-grid` rendered, the shop header's `cart-badge` reading "0" and `cart-link`'s accessible name reading "Kosár megnyitása, jelenleg üres": the new cart is empty, not the completed order's. - expect: Back returns the browser to the same `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` URL, where `order-receipt` renders again with its one `receipt-line` and `success-new-shopping` is offered — the receipt stays readable at its own URL, and Back never reopens the wizard or brings the ordered line back into the cart. - expect: Forward returns the browser to `/telefonok` with `cart-badge` still "0".
3. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-page` is visible in its empty state: the title "A kosarad még üres.", the message "Nézd meg a demókészülékeket, és válaszd ki a kedvenced." and the action "Telefonokat nézek". - expect: No `cart-line` is rendered and no `order-summary` is rendered, so no line of the completed order is offered for a second purchase.
4. Deep-link the review step with the cart still empty: navigate to `/penztar/ellenorzes`. - expect: The review guard sends the empty cart back: the URL is `/penztar/kosar?navigationReason=empty-cart` and `cart-page` is visible. - expect: `navigation-reason` reads "A kosarad üres, ezért ide irányítottunk." with a "Bezárás" control, and `review-page` is not rendered.
5. Start the new purchase: navigate to `/telefonok`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, press the add control of the mount the viewport shows, and open the cart with `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-badge` reads "1" and the cart holds only this line: exactly one `cart-line`, its `cart-line-name` the phone just added, `cart-line-quantity` "1 db" and `cart-count` "Kosárban található termékek (1)". - expect: The completed order's product is not among the lines: this cart holds one line only, the one added after the new shopping started.
6. Deep-link the review step again, now with a cart holding a line: navigate to `/penztar/ellenorzes`. - expect: The review guard cannot read the completed draft, so it sends the shopper to the earliest step that is missing: the URL is `/penztar/adatok?navigationReason=incomplete` and `contact-page` is visible. - expect: `navigation-reason` reads "Előbb ezt a lépést kell kitöltened, utána léphetsz tovább." and `review-page` is not rendered — the completed order's contact, delivery and review data are gone, so its lines can never be ordered again in one step.

#### 1.5. [E104] the header badge after a completed receipt

**File:** `e2e/integration/cart-story-badge-after-receipt.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Reach a receipt with the cash-on-delivery walk of `specs/storefront-integration.md` scenario 3.7: open `/telefonok?sort=price_asc`, pick the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker whose `price-amount` is at most 300 000 Ft, open it through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the add control of the mount the viewport shows, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`, then on the delivery step choose "Házhoz szállítás", fill "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", choose "Utánvét", press `delivery-confirm`, tick `review-consent-terms-input` and press the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` with `order-receipt` visible and `success-heading` reading "Elkészült a demórendelésed.". - expect: The receipt lists exactly one `receipt-line` (the ordered phone, "1 db") and `receipt-row-total` shows the order's "Végösszeg" — the committed items the header badge must not count.
2. Inspect the receipt page's header. - expect: The header is the checkout header `checkout-header`: it carries the brand, the "Demó pénztár" label and `back-to-shop` ("Vissza a shopba"), and the badged shop header `main-header` is not rendered. - expect: `cart-badge` is not rendered on this page at all — the badge is absent here rather than showing the ordered items, so it is inspected on the shop screen the new-shopping action lands on.
3. Press the inner button of `success-new-shopping` ("Vissza a telefonokhoz"). - expect: The browser is on `/telefonok` with `catalog-grid` rendered and the shop header `main-header` back. - expect: `cart-badge` reads "0" and `cart-link`'s accessible name reads "Kosár megnyitása, jelenleg üres" — the badge reflects the new, empty cart, not the one item the receipt still lists.
4. Add one phone: open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta` and press the add control of the mount the viewport shows (the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, `purchase-cta` below 1024 px). - expect: The confirmation reads "<product name>, <colour>, <n> GB – 1 darab a kosárban." - expect: `cart-badge` reads "1", so it counts only the phone added after the receipt and not the committed order's item.
5. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-page` holds exactly one `cart-line`, its `cart-line-name` the phone just added and its `cart-line-quantity` "1 db". - expect: `cart-count` reads "Kosárban található termékek (1)" and `order-summary-subtotal`'s `price-amount` is that line's price alone, so no committed item is counted anywhere on the screen. - expect: `cart-badge` is not rendered on the checkout layout's cart page, so the badge read above stands as the shop header's own count.
