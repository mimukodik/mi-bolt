# Receipt paths test plan

## Application Overview

MiBolt is a demo mobile-phone storefront (Angular, Hungarian UI). Its Playwright `seed` and `integration` projects serve the whole API from an in-page MSW mock, so no backend runs; the mock keeps its state in the browsing context's `sessionStorage` under `mi-bolt.snapshot.default` (a JSON document), those projects start signed in, and the default scenario starts with an empty cart. These two scenarios cover rows E101 and E102 of `docs/spec/test-scenarios.md` on the order-receipt surface at `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`. E101 ("Open valid success route") opens the success route from a completed demo purchase and reads the `DEMO` order number and the explicit no-real-order / no-real-payment / no-real-e-mail notices. E102 ("Reload success route") reloads that route and reads the same receipt back from a single `GET /api/v1/orders/<orderId>`, with no new `POST` to `/api/v1/orders` and with none of the typed personal data in the page text or in the `sessionStorage` snapshot. Both scenarios start from a fresh page in a fresh context and are written to be followed at 1920x1080 and at 390x844; the steps that differ by width say which mount applies where. Neither scenario names a product slug or a product name: the phone is picked by what the catalogue card shows.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. The receipt: a demo order, read back without personal data

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. [E101] the success page shows a DEMO order number and says no real order, payment or e-mail happened

**File:** `e2e/integration/receipt-demo-notice.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Start from a fresh page in a fresh browser context (the MSW projects start signed in, on the default scenario, with an empty cart) and open the catalogue: `await page.goto('/telefonok')`. - expect: The page title is `MiBolt — Mobiltelefonok` and `catalog-grid` is visible. - expect: The header's `cart-link` is visible and its badge `cart-badge` reads `0`, so the cart starts empty.
2. Scan the cards in `catalog-grid` for the first card that carries no `product-card-stock` element and whose `price-amount` is below 300 000 Ft. In the default scenario that is the grid's first card: its `price-amount` reads `89 990` and its price line reads `Egyszeri készülékár | 89 990 | Ft`. - expect: The chosen card carries no `product-card-stock` element; that marker, when a card has it, reads exactly `Elfogyott`, and such a card is skipped. - expect: The chosen card's shown price is `89 990 Ft`, below 300 000 Ft, and its `product-card-cta` reads `Megnézem`.
3. Press the chosen card's `product-card-cta`. - expect: The browser lands on the product route `/telefonok/<slug>?variant=<variantId>` and the title is `MiBolt — Termék`. - expect: `product-page` and `product-name` are visible, and `product-name` reads the same name the chosen card's heading showed (this plan never names the phone). - expect: `product-stock` reads `Raktáron a demó készletben`.
4. Add the phone at the width's own mount: at 1920x1080 press the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`; at 390x844 the `add-to-cart-button` mount is present but not visible, so press the inner button of the sticky rail's `purchase-cta` instead. Both buttons read `Kosárba teszem`; at 390x844 the rail shows `Készülékár | 89 990 | Ft`. - expect: The `status-message` of the mount just used reads `<the chosen card's name>, Zöld, 256 GB – 1 darab a kosárban.` and carries a `Bezárás` button: at 1920x1080 that is `add-to-cart`'s own `status-message`, at 390x844 the `purchase-rail`'s `status-message`. - expect: `cart-badge` reads `1`.
5. Press the header's `cart-link`. - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/kosar` and the title is `MiBolt — Kosár`; `cart-page` is visible. - expect: `cart-count` reads `KOSÁRBAN TALÁLHATÓ TERMÉKEK (1)` and there is exactly one `cart-line`, whose `cart-line-quantity` reads `1 db`. - expect: `order-summary-subtotal` reads `Részösszeg | 89 990 | Ft`, `order-summary-shipping` reads `Szállítás | A következő lépésben választod ki.` and `order-summary-total` reads `Végösszeg | — | Nincs megjeleníthető ár.`
6. Press the inner button of `cart-continue` (its text is `Tovább az adatokhoz`). - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/adatok` and the title is `MiBolt — Kapcsolattartási adatok`; the heading reads `Kapcsolattartási adatok`. - expect: The step list `checkout-steps` shows `Kosár` marked `Kész` and `Adatok` as the current step. - expect: The step's demo paragraph reads `Demó pénztár. Kérlek, ne használj valós személyes adatokat — ez egy működési bemutató, nem küldünk valódi rendelést vagy e-mailt.`
7. Fill the contact step: `contact-fullname-input` with `Teszt Elek`, `contact-email-input` with `teszt.elek@example.test`, `contact-phone-input` with `+36 30 123 4567`, then press the inner button of `contact-submit` (its text is `Tovább a szállításhoz`). - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/szallitas` and the title is `MiBolt — Átvétel és fizetés`; the heading reads `Átvétel és fizetés`.
8. Choose home delivery: select the delivery radio (`delivery-radio` inside the first `delivery-option`), whose accessible name is `Házhoz szállítás 0 Futárral, 1–2 munkanapon belül.` - expect: `address-form` appears with `address-input-postalCode`, `address-input-city` and `address-input-line1`. - expect: `summary-row-delivery` reads `Házhoz szállítás`.
9. Fill the address: `address-input-postalCode` with `1052`, `address-input-city` with `Budapest`, `address-input-line1` with `Váci utca 12.`. - expect: Each field holds the typed value; `address-input-line1` accepts a house number.
10. Choose cash on delivery: select the second `payment-option`, whose text is `Utánvét | 490 | Ft | Készpénzzel vagy kártyával a futárnál.` - expect: `summary-row-payment` reads `Utánvét` and `summary-row-fee` shows `490 Ft`.
11. Press the inner button of `delivery-confirm` (its text is `Tovább az ellenőrzéshez`). - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/ellenorzes` and the title is `MiBolt — Rendelés ellenőrzése`; the heading reads `Minden rendben?` - expect: `review-demo-notice` reads `Demó pénztár. A rendelés leadása nem küld valódi megrendelést, nem indít valódi fizetést, és nem küld e-mailt. Az adatok a böngésző memóriájában maradnak.` - expect: `review-contact` lists the typed values, with the phone normalised to `+36301234567` — the page that shows them is this review step, not the receipt. - expect: `review-items` reads `1 tétel`, the single `review-line` shows `1 × 89 990 Ft` and `Sor végösszeg | 89 990 | Ft`. - expect: The `review-summary` rows read `Részösszeg | 89 990 | Ft`, `Szállítási díj | 0 | Ft`, `Fizetési díj | 490 | Ft`, `Végösszeg | 90 480 | Ft`, and `review-payment` reads `Utánvét` with `review-delivery` reading `Házhoz szállítás`.
12. Accept the demo terms by checking `review-consent-terms-input` (its label reads `Elfogadom a demó rendelési feltételeit .`; the note beside the submit reads `A demó feltételek elfogadása szükséges a leadáshoz.`), then press `review-submit` (its text is `Demórendelés véglegesítése`). - expect: The browser lands on the success route `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`, where `<orderId>` is a UUID (the run recorded `/penztar/siker/d03f873e-03f8-4b39-a9df-f74d7e713a8b`), and the title is `MiBolt — Demórendelés elkészült`. - expect: Exactly one `POST` to `/api/v1/orders` is made while the order is placed and it answers `201 Created`; no later order write follows on this page load. - expect: All four `checkout-steps` entries read `Kész`.
13. Read the receipt values on the success page. - expect: `success-heading` reads `Elkészült a demórendelésed.` - expect: `receipt-order-number` reads `DEMO-000001`, labelled `Rendelésszám`, and `receipt-demo-badge` reads `DEMO`. - expect: `success-demo-notice` reads `Demó pénztár. A sikeroldal csupán a működést mutatja be — nem történt valódi fizetés, szállítás vagy e-mail-küldés.` - expect: `receipt-simulation-notice` reads `Demó figyelmeztetés. A demórendelés elkészült. Nem történt valódi fizetés, rendelésküldés vagy e-mail-küldés.` - expect: `receipt-lines` shows the heading `Termékek` with one `receipt-line`: the chosen card's phone name and `Zöld • 256 GB`, `Mennyiség | 1 db`, `Sor végösszeg | 89 990 | Ft`. - expect: `receipt-totals` shows the heading `Összegzés` with `Részösszeg | 89 990 | Ft`, `Szállítási díj | 0 | Ft`, `Fizetési díj | 490 | Ft`, `Végösszeg | 90 480 | Ft`. - expect: `receipt-row-payment` reads `Fizetési mód | Utánvét` and `receipt-row-delivery` reads `Szállítási mód | Házhoz szállítás`; `receipt-row-status` reads `Státusz | Demó rendelés visszaigazolva`. - expect: The receipt carries no contact or address block at all, and the page's demo banner (`demo-notice`) reads `DEMÓ` with `Fiktív termékek és árak. A vásárlás szimulált; nem történik valódi fizetés.`
14. Check the page text and the snapshot for the typed strings. - expect: The page and the snapshot hold none of the typed strings: `Teszt Elek`, `teszt.elek@example.test`, `+36 30 123 4567`, `+36301234567` (the phone as the review step normalises it) and `Váci utca 12.` The page text (1130 characters) and `sessionStorage['mi-bolt.snapshot.default']` (2455 characters) each hold zero of them. - expect: The snapshot holds the order, not the customer: its receipt's `contactEmail` is `redacted@example.invalid`, `billing.name` is `redacted`, and both stored addresses carry `postalCode` `0000` with `city` and `line1` `redacted`.

Nem történt valódi fizetés, rendelésküldés vagy e-mail-küldés.`

Check the reloaded page text and the `sessionStorage` snapshot for the typed strings. - expect: The page and the snapshot hold none of the typed strings: `Teszt Elek`, `teszt.elek@example.test`, `+36 30 123 4567`, `+36301234567` (the phone as the review step normalises it) and `Váci utca 12.` This holds both before and after the reload; the page text (1130 characters) and `sessionStorage['mi-bolt.snapshot.default']` (2455 characters) each hold zero of them. - expect: No address, no order id shown as customer data and no contact block on the receipt surface: the receipt renders `receipt-lines`, `receipt-totals` and `receipt-meta` rows only, and the page's demo banner (`demo-notice`) reads `DEMÓ` with `Fiktív termékek és árak. A vásárlás szimulált; nem történik valódi fizetés.`

Read the stored receipt inside the snapshot `mi-bolt.snapshot.default` (the mock's own persistence). - expect: The snapshot holds one receipt with `orderNumber` `DEMO-000001`, `status` `confirmed`, `paymentMethod` `cash_on_delivery`, `subtotal` 89990 HUF, `shippingFee` 0 HUF, `paymentFee` 490 HUF and `total` 90480 HUF, matching the on-screen totals. - expect: Its item carries `quantity` 1 and `lineTotal` 89990 HUF, and its notice is `A demórendelés elkészült.
