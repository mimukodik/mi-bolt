# MiBolt checkout service-fault test plan

## Application Overview

MiBolt is a Hungarian demo storefront: an Angular application served from the Playwright base URL with the in-page MSW mock behind it (no backend, no database). This plan covers the checkout wizard and the receipt while one service endpoint refuses: the cart `/penztar/kosar`, the contact step `/penztar/adatok`, the delivery and payment step `/penztar/szallitas`, the review step `/penztar/ellenorzes`, the receipt `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` and the return from the demo payment page at `/penztar`. The happy paths and the payment round trip are `specs/storefront-integration.md`, and none of them is repeated here.

Three facts govern every scenario. First, each test starts in a fresh browser context, and the mock keeps its state (cart, checkout draft, payment intents, receipts) in that context's `sessionStorage` under a key that carries the scenario name, so one fresh context is an empty cart and the scenarios are independent. Second, the mock reads `?mi-scenario=<name>` when the document loads and the MSW worker starts once per document: an in-app navigation keeps the scenario although the address bar loses the query, while a `page.goto` without the query loads the default scenario and a _different, empty_ cart, so a `page.goto` that has to stay inside the fault carries the query again. Third, `?mi-scenario=fail-<operation>` answers that one endpoint with a 503 problem every time and `?mi-scenario=offline-<operation>` makes it fail as a network error, while every other endpoint works. The two flavours are the same fault to the shopper wherever a scenario says so: the screens here render the server's own English detail in the receipt's reload error alone, and the review step's failure banner differs between them in its `data-error-code` attribute only.

Products are never named by slug or product name: every scenario picks its card by what the screen shows, the first card with no "Elfogyott" marker. Controls built on the `app-button` primitive are host elements carrying the test id, so a press targets the native `button` inside them: `page.getByTestId('<id>').getByRole('button')`. Prices and totals are read from the `app-price-block` primitive, whose digits sit in `price-amount` with the `Ft` glyph as a separate span beside them.

The demo payment application at `/demo-fizetes/<paymentIntentId>` is a separate document that the scenario query does not reach, so no scenario asserts what it renders; the shop's return leg is opened by the plan's own URL, which does carry the query.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Checkout: service faults

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. the order submission is refused with a 503 and the whole draft stays on screen

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-order-submission-503.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=fail-order` and wait for the grid. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: `cart-badge` reads "0": a fresh context holds an empty cart under this scenario too.
2. Open the first `product-card` that has no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, then press the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` ("Kosárba teszem"). - expect: `product-page` is visible and `product-stock` reads "Raktáron a demó készletben". - expect: The confirmation names the product, its colour and storage and "1 darab a kosárban.", and `cart-badge` reads "1".
3. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link`, then press `cart-continue` ("Tovább az adatokhoz"). - expect: `cart-page` shows exactly one `cart-line` before the press. - expect: `contact-page` is visible on `/penztar/adatok` and its `h1` reads "Kapcsolattartási adatok".
4. Fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press the inner button of `contact-submit` ("Tovább a szállításhoz"). - expect: The wizard advances: `delivery-step` is visible on `/penztar/szallitas` and its `h1` reads "Átvétel és fizetés". - expect: The section write (`checkout-update`) goes through, because it is not the endpoint this scenario breaks.
5. On the delivery step choose "Házhoz szállítás" by its `delivery-radio`, fill "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", keep the billing "same as delivery" tick, choose "Utánvét" by its `payment-radio`, and press `delivery-confirm` ("Tovább az ellenőrzéshez"). - expect: `review-page` is visible on `/penztar/ellenorzes` and its `h1` reads "Minden rendben?". - expect: `review-payment` reads "Utánvét", `review-delivery` names "Házhoz szállítás", `review-items` lists one `review-line` and `review-summary` is rendered. - expect: With `review-consent-terms-input` ticked, the inner button of `review-submit` ("Demórendelés véglegesítése") is enabled and no `review-cta-explanation` is rendered.
6. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` ("Elfogadom a demó rendelési feltételeit.") and press the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The browser stays on `/penztar/ellenorzes`: no navigation happens and `order-receipt` is not rendered. - expect: `review-submit-failure` is rendered, its `data-error-code` reads "SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE", its message reads "A kosarad és az adataid megmaradtak. Próbáld újra, vagy módosítsd a fizetési módot.", and it carries a "Fizetési mód módosítása" control (`review-change-payment`). - expect: The step's own banner `review-state-banner` reads "A rendelés leadása nem sikerült.". - expect: Nothing was lost: `review-contact` still shows "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567", `review-delivery` still names "Házhoz szállítás" and "Utánvét", `review-items` still lists the ordered line, `review-summary` is still rendered, and the terms tick is still checked.
7. Press the inner button of `review-submit` again. - expect: The same failure returns: `review-submit-failure` is rendered again with the same sentence, the same `data-error-code` and the same "Fizetési mód módosítása" control. - expect: The browser is still on `/penztar/ellenorzes` and the review content is unchanged, so the repeated attempt costs nothing.
8. Press `review-change-payment` ("Fizetési mód módosítása"). - expect: The browser is on `/penztar/szallitas` with `delivery-step` visible and `review-page` gone: the failure's way out leads back to the delivery step.

#### 1.2. the network error on the order submission shows the same failure with another error code

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-order-submission-offline.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Reach the review page with a cash-on-delivery draft under the network fault: open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=offline-order`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`, then on the delivery step choose "Házhoz szállítás", fill "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", choose "Utánvét" by its `payment-radio` and press `delivery-confirm`. - expect: `review-page` is visible on `/penztar/ellenorzes`, `review-payment` reads "Utánvét" and `review-items` lists one `review-line`. - expect: The wizard's own header carries the step indicator `checkout-steps` and the checkout layout shows no `cart-badge`.
2. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` and press the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The browser stays on `/penztar/ellenorzes`: `order-receipt` is not rendered. - expect: `review-submit-failure` is rendered, its `data-error-code` reads "UNKNOWN", and its message reads "A kosarad és az adataid megmaradtak. Próbáld újra, vagy módosítsd a fizetési módot." with a "Fizetési mód módosítása" control beside it. - expect: `review-state-banner` reads "A rendelés leadása nem sikerült.". - expect: The on-screen copy is what the 503 flavour shows too: the two differ only in `data-error-code` ("UNKNOWN" here against "SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE" there) and in what failed under the request, so a shopper cannot tell the two faults apart on this screen.
3. Press the inner button of `review-submit` once more and read the page again. - expect: The same sentence, the same `data-error-code` "UNKNOWN" and the same state banner return, and the browser never leaves `/penztar/ellenorzes`. - expect: The draft is intact after both attempts: `review-contact`, `review-delivery`, `review-items` and `review-summary` are still rendered.

#### 1.3. a deep link to the delivery or review step bounces to the contact step when the draft cannot be read

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-checkout-draft-read.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=fail-checkout`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`. - expect: `delivery-step` is visible on `/penztar/szallitas`: the contact section is written, so the draft does carry it although the draft read (`checkout`) answers 503.
2. Open the delivery step as a fresh page load inside the fault: navigate to `/penztar/szallitas?mi-scenario=fail-checkout`. - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/adatok?navigationReason=incomplete` and `contact-page` is visible: the delivery step's guard could not read the draft, so it sends the shopper to the earliest step it cannot confirm. - expect: `navigation-reason` reads "Előbb ezt a lépést kell kitöltened, utána léphetsz tovább." and carries a "Bezárás" control. - expect: The contact form is empty — `contact-fullname-input`, `contact-email-input` and `contact-phone-input` hold nothing — although the section was saved in the first step: the form is seeded from the draft the page could not load. - expect: `delivery-step` is not rendered and `error-state` is not rendered either: the delivery page is never reached.
3. Open the review step the same way: navigate to `/penztar/ellenorzes?mi-scenario=fail-checkout`. - expect: The same landing: `/penztar/adatok?navigationReason=incomplete` with `contact-page` visible and `navigation-reason` reading "Előbb ezt a lépést kell kitöltened, utána léphetsz tovább.". - expect: `review-page` is not rendered, so no step past the contact form is reachable while the draft read fails.
4. Fill the contact form again with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: The wizard advances to `/penztar/szallitas` with `delivery-step` visible and no `navigation-reason`: the section write puts the session back in memory for this document. - expect: The wizard's own header still carries the step indicator `checkout-steps`, so the shopper is inside the same checkout session the cart was handed over to.

#### 1.4. a refused checkout step save keeps the shopper on the contact step

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-checkout-step-save.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=fail-checkout-update`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, then fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: The browser stays on `/penztar/adatok`: `delivery-step` is not rendered and the wizard does not advance. - expect: `contact-save-error` is rendered and reads "A mentés sikertelen. Próbáld újra.", with no "Bezárás" control: a refused step save is a persistent error, not a dismissible notice. - expect: Nothing was lost: `contact-fullname-input` holds "Teszt Elek", `contact-email-input` holds "teszt@example.test" and `contact-phone-input` holds "+36 30 123 4567". - expect: Each field's wrapper carries `data-state="server-error"` and the inner button of `contact-submit` is enabled again. - expect: `contact-summary` is not rendered: the server answered 503 without per-field errors, so no field is named.
2. Press the inner button of `contact-submit` again. - expect: The same sentence returns in `contact-save-error`, the field values are unchanged and the browser still does not advance: the fault is permanent for this document. - expect: The step stays usable: `contact-submit` is enabled and no input carries `aria-invalid="true"`.

#### 1.5. the delivery step cannot load its delivery and payment options

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-checkout-options.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=fail-checkout-options`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`. - expect: The browser is on `/penztar/szallitas` and the contact section was saved, so the wizard advanced on its own. - expect: `delivery-step` is not rendered, and neither is `delivery-loading`: the options read has finished and failed. - expect: `error-state` is shown with the title "Az opciók nem érhetők el", the message "A szállítási és fizetési opciók betöltése sikertelen." and one control reading "Újrapróbálom". - expect: No `address-form`, no `delivery-radio` and no `payment-radio` are rendered: the step shows only what the server did not tell it, which is nothing.
2. #### 1.6. the receipt cannot be read back from the server

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-receipt-reload.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Place a cash-on-delivery order under the fault and read the receipt: open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=fail-order-read`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`, then on the delivery step choose "Házhoz szállítás", fill "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", choose "Utánvét" by its `payment-radio`, press `delivery-confirm`, tick `review-consent-terms-input` and press the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` with an order id in the path, because the order submission (`order`) is not the endpoint this scenario breaks. - expect: `success-page` and `order-receipt` are visible and `success-heading` reads "Elkészült a demórendelésed.". - expect: `receipt-order-number` is not empty and `receipt-row-payment` names "Utánvét". - expect: Note the order id the address bar carries: the next step reopens exactly that URL.
2. Open the receipt again as a fresh page load inside the fault: navigate to `/penztar/siker/<orderId>?mi-scenario=fail-order-read` with the order id read from the address bar. - expect: `success-heading` reads "A betöltés nem sikerült" and `success-error` is visible. - expect: `success-error`'s title reads "A visszatöltés nem sikerült", its message reads "Simulated failure (fail-order-read scenario)" — the mock's own English detail is what this screen renders — and its single control reads "Visszatöltés újra". - expect: `order-receipt`, `receipt-order-number` and `success-actions` are not rendered: the page never invents a receipt it could not read. - expect: This is the one screen where the two flavours of a fault part ways on screen: the network error's message is the request's own "Http failure response for ...: 0 undefined" instead of the detail above, while the title and the control read the same.
3. Press "Visszatöltés újra". - expect: The same error state returns with the same title, message and control, and `order-receipt` never appears. - expect: The browser stays on `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`, and the retry re-reads rather than re-posts, so no second order is ever placed from here.

#### 1.7. the card payment cannot be started

**Steps:**

1. 2. #### 1.8. the return from the payment page cannot read the intent

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-fault-payment-return-read.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Reach the review page with a card draft under the fault: open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=fail-payment-intent-read`, open the first `product-card` with no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add it with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, open the cart with `cart-link`, press `cart-continue`, fill the contact step with "Teszt Elek", "teszt@example.test" and "+36 30 123 4567" and press `contact-submit`, then on the delivery step choose "Házhoz szállítás", fill "1052" / "Budapest" / "Fő utca 1.", choose "Bankkártya" by its `payment-radio` and press `delivery-confirm`. - expect: `review-page` is visible on `/penztar/ellenorzes` with `review-payment` reading "Bankkártya" and `review-payment-new` offering "Fizetés indítása".
2. Press the inner button of `review-payment-new` and read the address bar. - expect: The intent is created — the create (`payment-intent`) is not the endpoint this scenario breaks — and the browser leaves the shop for the demo payment application: the URL is `/demo-fizetes/<paymentIntentId>` with the intent id as its last path segment. - expect: Read nothing into the demo payment page's own copy: it is a separate document that the scenario query does not reach.
3. Open the shop's return leg as a fresh page load inside the fault: navigate to `/penztar?mi-scenario=fail-payment-intent-read` (the page reads the intent id the shop stored when the intent was created). - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/ellenorzes` with `review-page` visible. - expect: `review-payment-status` reads "A fizetési szándék nem található." and `review-payment-new` is labelled "Új fizetés indítása": the read failed, so the shop cannot tell the shopper what became of the payment. - expect: No `review-submit-failure` and no `review-state-banner` are rendered, so the failure arrives as the payment branch's own sentence rather than as an order failure. - expect: The inner button of `review-submit` is disabled and `review-cta-explanation` reads "A kártyás fizetés jóváhagyása szükséges a rendelés leadásához.".
4. Press the inner button of `review-payment-new` ("Új fizetés indítása"). - expect: A new intent is created and the browser leaves the shop again: the URL is `/demo-fizetes/<paymentIntentId>` with an id other than the one read in the previous step. - expect: The way out is a fresh payment rather than a replay, so the shopper is never sent back to the intent the shop could not read.
