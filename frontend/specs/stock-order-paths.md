# Stock-order paths (E085, E099)

## Application Overview

MiBolt storefront (Angular 22 running on the in-page MSW mock at the Playwright baseURL, signed in by default). The two stories cover the sold-out-at-confirm edge: under the query `?mi-scenario=stock-lost-once-order`, read once at the first page load, the first order request of the page load takes the whole stock of the cart's last line (another buyer took it) and the order is refused with HTTP 409 OUT_OF_STOCK. The mock state and the cart/checkout draft live in the page (MSW handlers plus sessionStorage), so each story starts from a fresh browser context and never reloads; after the first load every step moves by clicking. Phones are picked by what the screen shows (position in the price-ascending list, the `product-card-stock` marker, the price), never by slug or name. Every story is run twice, at 1920x1080 and at 390x844; the only width-dependent control is the add-to-cart affordance (the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, the sticky rail's `purchase-cta` below that) plus the way back to the catalogue from a product page (the header's "Mobiltelefonok" link at 1024 px and wider, the `menu-trigger` dialog below it).

## Test Scenarios

### 1. An order refused because stock ran out meanwhile

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. [E085] an order refused at confirm for lost stock goes back to the cart and keeps the contact data

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-edge-sold-out-at-confirm.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Start from a fresh browser context (no stored cart or checkout snapshot) and open the path `/telefonok?sort=price_asc&mi-scenario=stock-lost-once-order` relative to the Playwright baseURL. This is the first and only page load: the mock reads `mi-scenario` here, so every later step moves by clicking and the page is never reloaded (a reload re-arms the mock and restores the stock). Run the story twice, at 1920x1080 and at 390x844. - expect: `result-count` reads `55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint`. - expect: `catalog-sort` shows the selected option `Ár szerint növekvő`. - expect: `cart-badge` reads `0`. - expect: The 12 cards of page 1 are in ascending price order; only the last one (159 990 Ft) carries a `product-card-stock` marker, reading `Elfogyott`.
2. Click the `product-card-cta` of the first card that has no `product-card-stock` marker (the 69 990 Ft card at the top of the list). - expect: The product page opens and `product-stock` reads `Raktáron a demó készletben`. - expect: At 1920x1080 the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` (`Kosárba teszem`) is visible and enabled while `purchase-cta` is hidden; at 390x844 `add-to-cart-button` is hidden while the inner button of `purchase-cta` (`Kosárba teszem`) is visible and enabled.
3. Click the add control for the width: the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, the inner button of `purchase-cta` below. - expect: `cart-badge` reads `1`.
4. Click `cart-link`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/kosar`. - expect: `cart-count` reads `Kosárban található termékek (1)` and there is exactly one `cart-line`, showing `1 db` and 69 990 Ft. - expect: `cart-continue` is present and enabled, labelled `Tovább az adatokhoz`. - expect: No `cart-notice` is on the page.
5. Click `cart-continue`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/adatok`. - expect: `contact-fullname-input`, `contact-email-input` and `contact-phone-input` are all empty.
6. Fill `contact-fullname-input` with `Teszt Elek`, `contact-email-input` with `teszt@example.test`, `contact-phone-input` with `+36 30 123 4567`, then click `contact-submit`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/szallitas`.
7. Click the first `delivery-radio` (the `Házhoz szállítás` option, 0 Ft). - expect: The address group appears with `address-input-postalCode`, `address-input-city`, `address-input-line1` and `address-input-line2`, all empty. - expect: The summary's `Szállítási mód` row now reads `Házhoz szállítás`.
8. Fill `address-input-postalCode` with `1052`, `address-input-city` with `Budapest`, `address-input-line1` with `Fő utca 1.`, click the second `payment-radio` (the `Utánvét` option, 490 Ft), then click `delivery-confirm`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/ellenorzes`. - expect: `review-line` count is 1. - expect: `order-summary-total` shows `Végösszeg` with 70 480 Ft.
9. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` and click the inner button of `review-submit` (`Demórendelés véglegesítése`). - expect: The URL stays `/penztar/ellenorzes` and never reaches `/penztar/siker/`. - expect: `order-receipt` count is 0. - expect: `review-state-banner` reads `Egy vagy több termék közben elfogyott, ezért a rendelés nem jött létre. A kosárban javíthatod; a megadott adataid megmaradnak.` and holds `review-stock-back-to-cart`, whose inner button is labelled `Vissza a kosárhoz`. - expect: `cart-badge` count is 0 here: the checkout header shows `DEMÓ PÉNZTÁR` and the `back-to-shop` link `Vissza a shopba` instead of the shop header. - expect: The order call answers HTTP 409: exactly one failed `POST /api/v1/orders` in the network log.
10. Click the inner button of `review-stock-back-to-cart`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/kosar`. - expect: The refused line is not shown as a line any more: `cart-line` count is 0 and `cart-count` is absent. - expect: `cart-notice` reads `<the phone's name> kikerült a kosárból, mert már nem kapható.`, naming the 69 990 Ft phone the cart held. - expect: `empty-state` reads `A kosarad még üres.` with the message `Nézd meg a demókészülékeket, és válaszd ki a kedvenced.` and the action button `Telefonokat nézek`. - expect: `cart-badge` count is 0; the cart page offers no line to remove and no `cart-continue`.
11. Correct the cart the way the page offers: click the `empty-state` action `Telefonokat nézek`, set `catalog-sort` to `Ár szerint növekvő`, click the `product-card-cta` of the first card without a `product-card-stock` marker, add it with the add control for the width, then click `cart-link`. - expect: `cart-count` reads `Kosárban található termékek (1)`. - expect: `cart-continue` is present and enabled. - expect: No `cart-notice` is left on the page.
12. Click `cart-continue`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/adatok`. - expect: The three contact inputs still hold the values typed before the refusal: `contact-fullname-input` = `Teszt Elek`, `contact-email-input` = `teszt@example.test`, `contact-phone-input` = `+36301234567`.

#### 1.2. [E099] a refused order leaves no receipt, no used order number and no half-ordered cart

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-edge-refused-order-leaves-nothing.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Start from a fresh browser context (no stored cart or checkout snapshot) and open the path `/telefonok?sort=price_asc&mi-scenario=stock-lost-once-order` relative to the Playwright baseURL. This is the first and only page load; every later step moves by clicking and the page is never reloaded. Run the story twice, at 1920x1080 and at 390x844. - expect: `result-count` reads `55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint`. - expect: `catalog-sort` shows `Ár szerint növekvő`. - expect: `cart-badge` reads `0`.
2. Click the `product-card-cta` of the first card without a `product-card-stock` marker (the 69 990 Ft card) and add it with the add control for the width (inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, inner button of `purchase-cta` below). - expect: `product-stock` on its product page reads `Raktáron a demó készletben`. - expect: `cart-badge` reads `1`.
3. Return to the catalogue by clicking: at 1024 px and wider the header link `Mobiltelefonok` inside `nav[aria-label="Fő navigáció"]`; below 1024 px `menu-trigger` and then the `Mobiltelefonok` link inside the `Menü` dialog's `Mobil navigáció` navigation. - expect: URL is `/telefonok` (the query string is gone from the URL; the scenario is already armed for this page load). - expect: `cart-badge` still reads `1`.
4. Set `catalog-sort` to `Ár szerint növekvő`, click the `product-card-cta` of the second card without a `product-card-stock` marker (the 89 990 Ft card) and add it with the add control for the width. - expect: `cart-badge` reads `2`. - expect: The cart's lines are the 69 990 Ft phone first and the 89 990 Ft phone last.
5. Click `cart-link`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/kosar`. - expect: `cart-count` reads `Kosárban található termékek (2)`. - expect: There are two `cart-line`s in the order added: the 69 990 Ft phone, then the 89 990 Ft phone, each showing `1 db`. - expect: `order-summary-subtotal` reads 159 980 Ft, which stays under the 300 000 Ft cash-on-delivery limit. - expect: `cart-continue` is present and enabled.
6. Click `cart-continue`, fill `contact-fullname-input` with `Teszt Elek`, `contact-email-input` with `teszt@example.test`, `contact-phone-input` with `+36 30 123 4567`, then click `contact-submit`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/adatok` after the cart, then `/penztar/szallitas` after the contact submit.
7. Click the first `delivery-radio` (`Házhoz szállítás`), fill `address-input-postalCode` with `1052`, `address-input-city` with `Budapest`, `address-input-line1` with `Fő utca 1.`, click the second `payment-radio` (`Utánvét`, 490 Ft), then click `delivery-confirm`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/ellenorzes`. - expect: `review-line` count is 2. - expect: `order-summary-total` shows `Végösszeg` with 160 470 Ft.
8. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` and click the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The URL stays `/penztar/ellenorzes` and never reaches `/penztar/siker/`. - expect: `order-receipt` count is 0. - expect: `review-state-banner` reads `Egy vagy több termék közben elfogyott, ezért a rendelés nem jött létre. A kosárban javíthatod; a megadott adataid megmaradnak.`, with `review-stock-back-to-cart` labelled `Vissza a kosárhoz`. - expect: `cart-badge` count is 0; the order call answers HTTP 409 (one failed `POST /api/v1/orders`).
9. Click the inner button of `review-stock-back-to-cart`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/kosar`. - expect: `cart-count` reads `Kosárban található termékek (1)`: the cart's stock reconcile drops the last line (the 89 990 Ft phone), so it is no longer a line. Record this count as the cart's own correction, not a half-order. - expect: `cart-notice` reads `<the dropped phone's name> kikerült a kosárból, mert már nem kapható.`, naming the 89 990 Ft phone. - expect: The one remaining `cart-line` is the 69 990 Ft phone, showing `1 db` and 69 990 Ft; no `order-receipt` exists and no order number is shown anywhere. - expect: `cart-continue` count is 1 and enabled; `cart-badge` count is 0. The remaining line keeps its own remove control (`cart-line-remove`, visible and enabled, labelled `Törlöm: <the kept phone's name>`) and `cart-clear` (`Kosár ürítése`) is on the page; what the reconcile removed is the dropped phone as a line.
10. Click `cart-continue` and walk the steps again: `contact-submit`, then `delivery-confirm`. - expect: At `/penztar/adatok` all three contact fields are prefilled: `contact-fullname-input` = `Teszt Elek`, `contact-email-input` = `teszt@example.test`, `contact-phone-input` = `+36301234567`. - expect: At `/penztar/szallitas` the address is prefilled (`address-input-postalCode` = `1052`, `address-input-city` = `Budapest`, `address-input-line1` = `Fő utca 1.`) and the first `delivery-radio` (`Házhoz szállítás`) and second `payment-radio` (`Utánvét`) are still selected. - expect: At `/penztar/ellenorzes` `review-line` count is 1 and `order-summary-total` shows `Végösszeg` with 70 480 Ft.
11. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` and click the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: URL is `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`, `<orderId>` being a UUID. - expect: `order-receipt` count is 1. - expect: `receipt-order-number` reads `DEMO-000001`: the refused attempt used up no order number. - expect: `receipt-line` count is 1 and it is the 69 990 Ft phone with `Mennyiség` `1 db` and `Sor végösszeg` 69 990 Ft. - expect: The receipt's summary rows read `Részösszeg` 69 990 Ft, `Szállítási díj` 0 Ft, `Fizetési díj` 490 Ft, `Végösszeg` 70 480 Ft, with `Fizetési mód` `Utánvét` and `Szállítási mód` `Házhoz szállítás`. - expect: `cart-badge` count is 0.
12. Open the dropped phone's page from the catalogue by clicking: click `back-to-shop` (`Vissza a shopba`), set `catalog-sort` to `Ár szerint növekvő`, then click the `product-card-cta` of the card whose `product-card-stock` marker reads `Elfogyott` (the 89 990 Ft card). - expect: In the catalogue that phone's card now carries `product-card-stock` reading `Elfogyott`: the other buyer's order took its stock.
