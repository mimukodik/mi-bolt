# Test Plan

## Application Overview

Two smoke scenarios that drive a full storefront purchase on the real backend at http://localhost:4301 (no MSW). Each journey starts at the phone listing, picks the first in-stock product, completes the checkout (contact, home delivery, same-as-delivery billing, terms), and either finishes on the receipt page directly (cash on delivery) or round-trips through the /demo-fizetes/ payment app (card) before landing back on the receipt. The product is chosen by stock label at run time so orders from earlier runs cannot exhaust it; the cash-on-delivery scenario additionally caps the chosen card by the 300 000 Ft backend ceiling (mibolt.checkout.cash-on-delivery-max-cart-huf=300000 at backend/services/commerce/src/main/resources/application.properties:46) so the Utánvét radio stays enabled at the delivery step. Both scenarios open /penztar/kosar first and clear any leftover lines so a stale cart cannot disable value-bounded payment options or reroute the review step into a leftover payment-intent branch.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Smoke: purchase on the real stack

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. cash on delivery purchase reaches the receipt

**File:** `e2e/smoke/cash-on-delivery-purchase-reaches-the-receipt.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Reset the cart so a previous run cannot leave lines behind. Open `/penztar/kosar`; if `cart-clear` exposes its inner button, count the `cart-line` elements, click the button, and wait for `cart-line` to reach 0 before continuing. The smoke stack keys the cart by the X-Demo-Session header, and the MCP runner and the dev server share a tab across runs; a non-empty cart at this point disables value-bounded options the rest of the test relies on (Utánvét is rejected when the cart exceeds its ceiling), so the suite empties the cart first and waits for the delete to land before continuing. - expect: cart-page is visible after the reset (whether the cart was empty to begin with or was cleared).
2. Navigate to `/telefonok`. The phone listing page renders a header for the `telefonok` category and a grid of product cards, each marked with the test id `product-card`. - expect: The h1 "Mobiltelefonok" is visible. - expect: At least one `product-card` element renders in the catalog grid.
3. Pick the first `product-card` that is in stock (no Elfogyott label) and whose card price is at most 300 000 Ft, the cash-on-delivery cart ceiling (mibolt.checkout.cash-on-delivery-max-cart-huf=300000 at backend/services/commerce/src/main/resources/application.properties:46). Read the price as digits from `<app-price-block data-testid="price-amount">` inside the card's `.price-row` and compare as a number. Open that card by clicking its `product-card-cta`. - expect: A card is picked: its `product-card-stock` element is absent and its card price is at most 300 000 Ft. - expect: `product-page` is visible after the click. - expect: `add-to-cart-button` is visible.
4. If the product page asks for a variant (e.g. storage capacity, colour), pick the first available variant before adding to cart. Skip this step when the product page shows no variant selector. - expect: Either a variant is selected in each visible `variant-color-group` / `variant-storage-group` (first enabled `button[data-testid^="variant-color-"]` or `button[data-testid^="variant-storage-"]`), or no variant selector is visible. - expect: The inner button of `add-to-cart-button` is enabled.
5. Click `add-to-cart-button`. The cart updates; the header shows the new item count. - expect: `cart-badge` is no longer "0" (the header badge refresh is not synchronous with the click, so the assertion only checks that the badge moved off the empty-state 0).
6. Open the cart page (cart icon / `cart-link` from header or add-to-cart toast). The cart page lists the added line. - expect: `cart-page` is visible. - expect: `cart-line` count is 1. - expect: `cart-continue` is visible.
7. Click `cart-continue` to leave the cart and enter the checkout flow. The checkout opens on the contact step. - expect: `contact-page` is visible. - expect: `contact-submit` is visible.
8. Fill the contact step: a full name (e.g. `Teszt Elek`), an e-mail address (e.g. `teszt@example.test`), and a Hungarian mobile phone number (e.g. `+36 30 123 4567` or `06301234567`; the schema normalises the national prefix to +36 before validating, so any documented form is accepted). Submit via `contact-submit`. The delivery step appears. - expect: `contact-fullname-input`, `contact-email-input` and `contact-phone-input` accept the entered values. - expect: `contact-submit` is enabled before the click. - expect: `delivery-step` is visible after the click.
9. On the delivery step, choose the home delivery option. Fill a valid Hungarian address: recipient name, country `Magyarország` (display-only, no input), city, street and house number, and a four-digit postal code (e.g. `1052`). Before picking Utánvét, verify its `payment-radio` is enabled (the backend rejects cash on delivery when the cart subtotal exceeds the ceiling, and the cart can hold a higher-priced variant than the card's from-price). Click the Utánvét `payment-option`. Confirm the delivery section via `delivery-confirm`. - expect: The first `delivery-option` is selected as the home delivery option. - expect: `address-input-postalCode`, `address-input-city` and `address-input-line1` accept the four-digit postal code and the rest of the required Hungarian fields. - expect: `billing-same-as-delivery` is checked (billing defaults to "same as delivery"). - expect: The Utánvét `payment-radio` is enabled before the click; the option row exposes `payment-unavailable-reason` when it is not. - expect: `delivery-confirm` is enabled. - expect: `review-page` is visible after the click.
10. On the review step, keep billing the same as delivery (no separate billing address is entered). The Utánvét / cash on delivery option was already picked on the delivery step; the payment summary shows `Utánvét`. Accept the terms via `review-consent-terms-input` (checkbox / switch). Submit the order via `review-submit`. - expect: `review-billing` is visible. - expect: `review-payment` contains the text "Utánvét". - expect: `review-consent-terms-input` is checked. - expect: `review-submit` is enabled and accepts the click.
11. The order is created and the browser is redirected to the receipt at `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`. Read the order number from `receipt-order-number` and the payment row from `receipt-row-payment`. The page is the `order-receipt` surface. - expect: The URL matches `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` where `<orderId>` is the backend-assigned id. - expect: `order-receipt` is visible. - expect: `receipt-order-number` is visible and not empty. - expect: `receipt-row-payment` contains the text "Utánvét".
