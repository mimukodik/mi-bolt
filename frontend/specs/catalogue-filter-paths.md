# MiBolt catalogue filter controls test plan

## Application Overview

MiBolt is a Hungarian demo storefront: an Angular application served from the Playwright base URL with the in-page MSW mock behind it (no backend, no database, no sign-in step — the demo gate starts signed in). This plan covers what a shopper sees while searching and filtering on the phone catalogue `/telefonok`, whose fixture holds 55 entries and whose first page renders 12 cards.

The same specs run in both integration viewports, so every facet step names the control of the mount its viewport shows. At 1024 px and wider the filters are the sidebar `filter-sidebar`, holding the panel `filter-panel` and applying every edit immediately. Below 1024 px the same filters are the drawer that `filter-trigger` opens, holding `filter-drawer` and its `drawer-*` controls, and its edits are a transient draft: nothing reaches the catalogue until `drawer-apply` is pressed, and the drawer closes on every apply (so a step that applies two drawer filters opens the drawer twice). Two panel controls exist at one width only and the scenarios that use them say so in their first step: the search field `filter-search-input` (the drawer carries no search field) and the reset `filter-reset`. The manufacturer and colour "more" controls keep the same test ids in both mounts — `filter-brand-more` and `filter-color-more` — the drawer's copies sitting inside `filter-drawer`.

Applied filters show as `applied-chip` elements inside `applied-filters`, above the grid and shared by both widths; each chip carries its own remove button `applied-chip-remove-<name>` and the chip row carries `applied-filters-clear`. The toolbar carries `result-count`, `filter-trigger`, `catalog-sort` and `catalog-page-size`. Controls built on the `app-button` primitive are host elements carrying the test id, so a press targets the native `button` inside them (`page.getByTestId('filter-reset').getByRole('button')`, `drawer-apply`, `drawer-cancel`); `filter-trigger`, the facet buttons and the price inputs are native elements and are pressed directly. Prices are formatted with a non-breaking space as the thousands separator ("Min. ár: 300 000 Ft", "300 000 Ft"). The search field is debounced, so a typed term reaches the catalogue shortly after typing pauses and assertions are made against `result-count` rather than against a keystroke.

Products are never named by slug or product name: every scenario reads what the screen shows. The search debounce is 300 ms and the panel's price validation refuses a negative bound and a minimum above the maximum, in both cases leaving the applied range untouched. Scenarios already covered elsewhere and deliberately not repeated here: filtering by one manufacturer, colour or storage bucket alone, sorting, the page size and the pager, a filter combination with no results, clearing all filters with the chip row's "Összes szűrő törlése", a URL that carries the state, and the drawer's draft and cancel (see `specs/storefront-integration.md` and `specs/shop-edge-paths.md`). Below 1024 px the scenarios here take their steps in the header search and the filter drawer, so the drawer's price refusal and its reset are exercised here.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Catalogue: filter controls

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. typing a search term narrows the list and clearing it restores the catalogue

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-search.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid: `result-count` names a number and at least one `product-card` is rendered. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and exactly 12 `product-card` elements are rendered (the default page size of 12). - expect: This scenario uses the panel's search field `filter-search-input`, which sits in the sidebar `filter-sidebar` shown at 1024 px and wider only: the filter drawer opened by `filter-trigger` carries no search field, so every step here is taken at 1024 px and wider.
2. Type "honor" into `filter-search-input`. - expect: The term reaches the catalogue shortly after typing pauses — the search is debounced, so the list updates on the pause rather than on the keystroke: `result-count` reads "8 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 8 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The URL query string carries `q=honor`. - expect: `applied-filters` appears with exactly one `applied-chip`, whose label reads "Keresés: „honor”" and whose remove button is `applied-chip-remove-q`. - expect: `filter-search-input` keeps the typed text. - expect: `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (1)" and the inner button of `filter-reset` is enabled.
3. Replace the term with "motorola" (type it over the current value, or clear the field and type it). - expect: The list narrows term by term: `result-count` reads "1 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and exactly one `product-card` is rendered. - expect: The chip's label now reads "Keresés: „motorola”" and the URL carries `q=motorola`. - expect: `filter-search-input` shows the new term.
4. Clear `filter-search-input`. - expect: The URL is `/telefonok` again and carries no `q`. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: `applied-filters` is no longer rendered at all, `filter-trigger` reads "Szűrők", and the inner button of `filter-reset` is disabled again.

#### 1.2. a price range narrows the list

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-price-range.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid, then read the price fields. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The price fields — `filter-min-price` and `filter-max-price` in the sidebar at 1024 px and wider, `drawer-min-price` and `drawer-max-price` in the drawer opened with `filter-trigger` below 1024 px — are both empty and carry the result set's own bounds as their placeholders: "69990" on the minimum field and "1095990" on the maximum field.
2. Type "300000" into the minimum price field (`filter-min-price`; below 1024 px type it into `drawer-min-price`, then press `drawer-apply`). - expect: `result-count` reads "35 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint". - expect: The URL query string carries `minPrice=300000`, and one `applied-chip` reads "Min. ár: 300 000 Ft" with the remove button `applied-chip-remove-minPrice`. - expect: The minimum field's placeholder follows the narrower set and becomes "319990"; the maximum field is still empty and still hints "1095990".
3. Type "600000" into the maximum price field (`filter-max-price`; below 1024 px `drawer-max-price`, then press `drawer-apply`). - expect: `result-count` reads "25 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The URL carries `minPrice=300000&maxPrice=600000`. - expect: `applied-filters` holds two `applied-chip` elements reading "Min. ár: 300 000 Ft" and "Max. ár: 600 000 Ft"; both `applied-chip-remove-minPrice` and `applied-chip-remove-maxPrice` are present. - expect: Both placeholders follow the narrowed set: "319990" and "599990". - expect: `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (2)". - expect: Every price the visible cards show in `price-amount`, read as a number, falls between 300 000 Ft and 600 000 Ft.
4. Clear the maximum price field (`filter-max-price`; below 1024 px empty `drawer-max-price`, then press `drawer-apply`). - expect: The upper bound is gone: `result-count` reads "35 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" again and the URL carries `minPrice=300000` alone. - expect: `applied-filters` holds the single chip "Min. ár: 300 000 Ft" and `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (1)". - expect: The maximum field's placeholder returns to the full ceiling "1095990".

#### 1.3. the panel refuses a price range it cannot apply

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-price-error.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok`, wait for the grid, and type the valid range "300000" into `filter-min-price` and "600000" into `filter-max-price`. - expect: `result-count` reads "25 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The two chips read "Min. ár: 300 000 Ft" and "Max. ár: 600 000 Ft", and the URL carries `minPrice=300000&maxPrice=600000`. - expect: The refusal this scenario tests is the panel's own: the message `filter-price-error` and the fields `filter-min-price` / `filter-max-price` live in the sidebar `filter-sidebar`, shown at 1024 px and wider only, so every step here is taken at that width.
2. Type "900000" into `filter-min-price`, a minimum above the maximum. - expect: `filter-price-error` reads "A minimum nem lehet nagyobb a maximum árnál." - expect: `filter-min-price` carries `aria-invalid="true"`; `filter-max-price` does not. - expect: Nothing is applied by the refused range: the URL still carries `minPrice=300000&maxPrice=600000`, `result-count` still reads "25 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint", and the two chips are unchanged. - expect: `filter-min-price` shows the typed "900000" while the applied range stays visible in the chips.
3. Correct the range by typing "400000" into `filter-min-price`. - expect: The message and `aria-invalid` are gone. - expect: The corrected range applies: the URL carries `minPrice=400000&maxPrice=600000`, `result-count` reads "15 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint", and the chips read "Min. ár: 400 000 Ft" and "Max. ár: 600 000 Ft". - expect: The minimum field's placeholder follows the narrower set: "419990".
4. Type "-5" into `filter-min-price`, a negative minimum. - expect: `filter-price-error` reads "A minimum ár nem lehet negatív." - expect: `filter-min-price` carries `aria-invalid="true"` again. - expect: The applied range is untouched: the URL still carries `minPrice=400000&maxPrice=600000`, `result-count` still reads "15 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint", and the two chips are unchanged.

#### 1.4. the in-stock and 5G switches filter the list

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-stock-and-5g.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: `applied-filters` is not rendered and `filter-trigger` reads "Szűrők". - expect: The two switches are reachable at both widths: `filter-inStock` ("Csak raktáron (55)") and `filter-is5g` ("Csak 5G (54)") in the sidebar at 1024 px and wider, `drawer-inStock` and `drawer-is5g` in the drawer opened with `filter-trigger` below 1024 px.
2. Switch "Csak raktáron" on: press `filter-inStock` in the sidebar, or `drawer-inStock` then `drawer-apply` in the drawer. - expect: The control carries `aria-pressed="true"` and shows its "✓" mark. - expect: The URL carries `inStock=true` and one `applied-chip` reads "Csak raktáron" (`applied-chip-remove-inStock`). - expect: `result-count` still reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint": every entry of the fixture is in stock, so this switch on its own narrows nothing. - expect: `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (1)".
3. Switch "Csak 5G" on as well (`filter-is5g`, or `drawer-is5g` then `drawer-apply`). - expect: Both controls carry `aria-pressed="true"` and their own labels read "Csak raktáron (54)" and "Csak 5G (54)": the facet counts follow the applied state. - expect: `result-count` reads "54 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The URL carries `inStock=true&is5g=true`. - expect: `applied-filters` holds two `applied-chip` elements reading "Csak raktáron" and "Csak 5G", and `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (2)".
4. Switch "Csak 5G" off again (`filter-is5g`, or `drawer-is5g` then `drawer-apply`). - expect: `filter-is5g` carries `aria-pressed="false"` and its "✓" mark is gone, while `filter-inStock` stays pressed. - expect: The URL carries `inStock=true` alone; the "Csak 5G" chip is gone and "Csak raktáron" stays. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (1)".
5. Switch "Csak raktáron" off too (`filter-inStock`, or `drawer-inStock` then `drawer-apply`). - expect: Both controls read `aria-pressed="false"`. - expect: The URL is `/telefonok` again, `applied-filters` is no longer rendered, and `filter-trigger` reads "Szűrők".

#### 1.5. the manufacturer and colour lists open behind their more controls

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-more-lists.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid, then read the "Gyártó" and "Szín" groups: `filter-group-brand` and `filter-group-color` in the sidebar at 1024 px and wider, `drawer-group-brand` and `drawer-group-color` in the drawer opened with `filter-trigger` below 1024 px. - expect: Each group shows five entries and one "more" control: `filter-brand-more` reading "Még több gyártó" with `aria-expanded="false"`, and `filter-color-more` reading "Még több szín". - expect: The five manufacturers are "Apple (17)" (`filter-brand-apple`), "Google Pixel (4)" (`filter-brand-google pixel`, the space is part of the test id), "HONOR (8)" (`filter-brand-honor`), "Motorola (1)" (`filter-brand-motorola`) and "Samsung (13)" (`filter-brand-samsung`); in the drawer the same five carry `drawer-brand-*` with the same values. - expect: The five colours are "Zöld (3)", "Fekete (35)", "Burgundi (3)", "Ezüst (4)" and "Gleccserszín (3)" (`filter-color-zold`, `filter-color-fekete`, `filter-color-burgundi`, `filter-color-ezust`, `filter-color-gleccserszin`; `drawer-color-*` in the drawer). - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and `filter-trigger` reads "Szűrők".
2. Press the manufacturer "more" control (`filter-brand-more`; in the drawer the control with that same test id inside `filter-drawer`). - expect: The manufacturer list grows from five entries to seven: `filter-brand-vivo` reading "Vivo (3)" and `filter-brand-xiaomi` reading "Xiaomi (9)" appear. - expect: The control now reads "Kevesebb gyártó" and carries `aria-expanded="true"`. - expect: Nothing is filtered by opening the list: the URL is still `/telefonok` and `result-count` still reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint".
3. Press the colour "more" control (`filter-color-more`; in the drawer the control with that same test id inside `filter-drawer`). - expect: The colour list grows from five chips to 32; `filter-color-grafit` reading "Grafit (4)" is one of the chips the collapsed list does not show. - expect: The control now reads "Kevesebb szín". - expect: Nothing is filtered by opening the list: the URL is still `/telefonok` and `result-count` still reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint".
4. Activate a manufacturer that only the opened list carries: press `filter-brand-vivo` (below 1024 px press `drawer-brand-vivo`, then `drawer-apply`). - expect: The URL carries `brand=vivo` and one `applied-chip` reads "vivo" (`applied-chip-remove-brand`). - expect: `result-count` reads "3 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and three `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The manufacturer list stays open at seven entries with its control still reading "Kevesebb gyártó", so the manufacturer the shopper picked stays on screen. - expect: `filter-brand-vivo` carries `aria-pressed="true"` (in the drawer, `drawer-brand-vivo` does when the drawer is reopened).
5. Press the manufacturer "more" control again, asking for the list to collapse. - expect: The list still shows seven entries and the control still reads "Kevesebb gyártó" with `aria-expanded="true"`: the selected `filter-brand-vivo` keeps the list open, so the picked manufacturer is not collapsed out of reach. - expect: The catalogue is unchanged: the URL still carries `brand=vivo` and `result-count` still reads "3 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint".
6. Deactivate `filter-brand-vivo` (below 1024 px `drawer-brand-vivo`, then `drawer-apply`). - expect: The URL is `/telefonok` again, no `applied-chip` is rendered, and `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint". - expect: With the deeper manufacturer gone the list is back to five entries and the control reads "Még több gyártó" with `aria-expanded="false"`.

#### 1.6. removing one applied chip keeps the other filters

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-chip-removal.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: `applied-filters` is not rendered and `filter-trigger` reads "Szűrők".
2. Apply three filters: the manufacturer `filter-brand-apple` (below 1024 px `drawer-brand-apple`, then `drawer-apply`), the storage `filter-storage-256` (below 1024 px `drawer-storage-256`, then `drawer-apply`) and the switch `filter-is5g` (below 1024 px `drawer-is5g`, then `drawer-apply`). - expect: The URL query string carries `brand=apple&storageGb=256&is5g=true`. - expect: `applied-filters` holds exactly three `applied-chip` elements, reading "apple", "256 GB" and "Csak 5G" in that order. - expect: Their remove buttons are `applied-chip-remove-brand`, `applied-chip-remove-storage` and `applied-chip-remove-is5g`. - expect: `result-count` reads "7 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and seven `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (3)".
3. Remove the manufacturer chip with `applied-chip-remove-brand`. - expect: The other two chips stay and the manufacturer chip is gone: `applied-filters` holds "256 GB" (`applied-chip-remove-storage`) and "Csak 5G" (`applied-chip-remove-is5g`) and no longer holds `applied-chip-remove-brand`. - expect: The URL carries `storageGb=256&is5g=true` and no `brand`. - expect: The list widens: `result-count` reads "27 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The manufacturer control the viewport shows is unselected — `filter-brand-apple` in the sidebar, `drawer-brand-apple` when the drawer is reopened — while the storage and switch controls still read `aria-pressed="true"` (`filter-storage-256` and `filter-is5g`, or `drawer-storage-256` and `drawer-is5g` in the reopened drawer). - expect: `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (2)".

#### 1.7. the panel's reset clears every filter at once

**File:** `e2e/integration/catalogue-control-panel-reset.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok` and wait for the grid. - expect: `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and 12 `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The same scenario runs at every width: at 1024 px and wider the filters live in the sidebar `filter-sidebar` and the panel's reset `filter-reset` ("Szűrők törlése") is reachable at once; below 1024 px the sidebar is hidden and the trigger opens the `filter-drawer`, whose own reset `drawer-reset` ("Szűrők törlése") sits inside the drawer and is initially disabled because the draft is empty. - expect: At 1024 px and wider the inner button of `filter-reset` is disabled; below 1024 px opening the drawer shows the inner button of `drawer-reset` disabled.
2. Apply three filters from the panel: type "honor" into `filter-search-input`, press `filter-inStock`, then press `filter-storage-256`. - expect: At 1024 px and wider the panel applies each edit at once. - expect: Below 1024 px the search field is the header's `mobile-search-input` (placeholder "Keresés a telefonok között…"), whose Enter navigates to `/telefonok?q=honor`, so the term's own 8-hit set and one `applied-chip` reading "Keresés: „honor”" land before the drawer opens; then `drawer-inStock` and `drawer-storage-256` set the draft, and `drawer-apply` ("Szűrők alkalmazása") commits it, closing the drawer. - expect: `result-count` reads "3 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint" and three `product-card` elements are rendered. - expect: The URL query string carries `q=honor&inStock=true&storageGb=256`. - expect: `applied-filters` holds three `applied-chip` elements reading "Keresés: „honor”", "256 GB" and "Csak raktáron", and `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (3)". - expect: The reset the viewport shows is enabled: the inner button of `filter-reset` at 1024 px and wider, the inner button of `drawer-reset` after the drawer is reopened below 1024 px.
3. Type "-5" into `filter-min-price`, a range the panel refuses. - expect: At 1024 px and wider the panel shows the fault on its own `filter-price-error`; below 1024 px the drawer is opened again first, and the drawer's own `drawer-price-error` carries the same message "A minimum ár nem lehet negatív." - expect: Nothing is applied by it: the URL and the three chips are unchanged, and `result-count` still reads "3 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint".
4. Press the inner button of `filter-reset` ("Szűrők törlése"). - expect: At 1024 px and wider the reset commits at once: every filter is cleared, the URL is `/telefonok` carrying none of `q`, `inStock` and `storageGb`, `applied-filters` is no longer rendered, `result-count` reads "55 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint", 12 `product-card` elements are rendered, and `filter-trigger` reads "Szűrők". - expect: Below 1024 px the drawer's reset clears the draft only: the drawer stays open, the draft's controls are back to their defaults (`drawer-inStock` and `drawer-storage-256` carry `aria-pressed="false"`, `drawer-min-price` is empty, the `drawer-price-error` is gone, and the inner button of `drawer-reset` is disabled again), nothing has been committed yet (the URL, the three chips and the 3-hit counter are the applied state's), and only `drawer-apply` then commits the cleared draft: the drawer closes, `inStock` and `storageGb` are gone from the URL while `q=honor` stays, `applied-filters` holds the single chip "Keresés: „honor”", `result-count` reads "8 találat a szűrt feltételek szerint", and `filter-trigger` reports "Szűrők (1)"; the term is then removed with `applied-chip-remove-q`, taking the catalogue back to the bare set the wide path ends on. - expect: At 1024 px and wider the panel's own controls are back to their defaults: `filter-search-input` is empty, `filter-inStock` and `filter-storage-256` carry `aria-pressed="false"`, and the inner button of `filter-reset` is disabled again. - expect: The refused draft goes with the filters at every width: at 1024 px and wider `filter-min-price` is empty and `filter-price-error` is gone; below 1024 px the drawer's `drawer-min-price` is empty and `drawer-price-error` is gone, so no stale text is left behind the cleared catalogue.
