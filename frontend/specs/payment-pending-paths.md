# MiBolt storefront: pending card payment poll

## Application Overview

Two scenarios from the storefront acceptance catalogue (rows E094 and E095 of `frontend/docs/spec/test-scenarios.md`) that exercise the review step's poll of a pending card payment. The shop's MSW mock on `/api/v1/payment-intents` is the only API under test; the storefront boots signed in via `frontend/e2e/seed.spec.ts`. Each scenario runs once at desktop (1920x1080) and once at mobile (390x844).

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Card payment: a pending result is polled

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. [E094] a pending card payment is polled until it succeeds and exactly one order is placed

**File:** `e2e/integration/payment-pending-resolves.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok?mi-scenario=payment-pending-resolves` at the integration viewport (1920x1080 on the desktop pass, 390x844 on the mobile pass). Wait for the first `product-card` to render. - expect: The heading "Mobiltelefonok" is visible at level 1.
2. Pick the first catalogue card that carries no `product-card-stock` marker (the only on-screen signal of an in-stock variant), and click its inner link through `product-card-cta`. - expect: The product page loads with `product-page` visible and the in-stock label "Raktáron a demó készletben" inside `product-stock`.
3. Add the variant to the cart through the add control the viewport exposes: at 1920x1080 press the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`, at 390x844 press the inner button of `purchase-cta` (the sticky `purchase-rail` carries the press below 1024px). - expect: The header `cart-badge` reaches "1" once the add has landed.
4. Open the cart with `cart-link`, press the inner button of `cart-continue`, and on the contact step fill `contact-fullname-input` with "Teszt Elek", `contact-email-input` with "teszt@example.test" and `contact-phone-input` with "+36 30 123 4567". Press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: The URL becomes `…/penztar/szallitas` and the delivery step is visible under `delivery-step`.
5. On the delivery step choose the radio of the `delivery-option` whose text reads "Házhoz szállítás" (the inner radio is `delivery-radio`), fill `address-input-postalCode` with "1052", `address-input-city` with "Budapest" and `address-input-line1` with "Fő utca 1.", then click the `payment-option` whose text reads "Bankkártya" (the inner radio is `payment-radio`). Press the inner button of `delivery-confirm`. - expect: The URL becomes `…/penztar/ellenorzes` and the review step's `review-page` is visible. `review-payment` reads "Bankkártya", `review-payment-status` reads "A kártyás fizetéshez indítsd el a fizetést." and the inner button of `review-payment-new` reads "Fizetés indítása".
6. Arm the request recorder before the next press: install a listener on `page.on('request', …)` that captures the HTTP method and URL of every call to `/api/v1/payment-intents` and `/api/v1/orders` (the MSW handler answers these through the service worker, so the recorder sits on the CDP `Network.requestWillBeSent` surface, not on `window.fetch`). The recorder must already be installed before the page load that lands on `/penztar/ellenorzes` with the pending payment. - expect: The recorder's array starts empty (a single element pushed at most would be the cold-start read that follows).
7. Press the inner button of `review-payment-new`. - expect: The browser navigates to `/demo-fizetes/<paymentIntentId>` (a separate document under the demo payment app). The recorder captures exactly one new entry: `POST /api/v1/payment-intents` returning 201, with the Location header pointing at the new intent.
8. On the demo payment page, observe the card: the `pay-ref` heading names the transaction, `pay-amount` shows the same digits as the review step's `order-summary-total` `price-amount`, and the three outcome buttons `pay-succeeded` ("Sikeres fizetés"), `pay-declined` ("Elutasított fizetés") and `pay-pending` ("Függőben marad") are visible together with the four scenery fields `pay-card-number`, `pay-card-holder`, `pay-card-expiry`, `pay-card-cvc`. - expect: The screen shows the `pay-ref`, `pay-amount` (digits equal to the review total) and the three outcome buttons.
9. Press `pay-pending`. The demo payment page POSTs `…/payment-intents/<id>/outcome` with `{ outcome: 'pending' }` and then calls `window.location.assign(intent.returnUrl)`, which is `/penztar?paymentIntentId=<id>` — note that the URL carries no `?mi-scenario=` query, so the mock's scenario state on this fresh document falls back to the default. - expect: The browser navigates to `/penztar/ellenorzes` through the transient return URL `/penztar?paymentIntentId=<id>`; the recorder captures `POST …/payment-intents/<id>/outcome` returning 200 (followed by the cold-start `GET …/payment-intents/<id>` from the cold-loaded review step).
10. Observe the cold-loaded review step and confirm what the return leg lost: `review-payment-status` reads "Az eredmény még nem ismert, ezért nem tekintjük elutasítottnak." (the unknown-pending line), `review-payment-polling` is shown reading "Az állapotot másodpercenként automatikusan ellenőrizzük." and `review-payment-check` carries a button reading "Állapot ellenőrzése" under it. Then re-enter the shop's return leg as a fresh page load of `/penztar?mi-scenario=payment-pending-resolves` — the shop reads the payment intent id from `sessionStorage` (`mi-bolt.checkout.intent-id`), and the URL carries the scenario back so the mock's `payment-pending-resolves` behaviour (a pending intent answers its reads as pending until the third read in one page load, which answers it as succeeded) applies to every GET the storefront issues from this point. - expect: The landing URL is `/penztar/ellenorzes`. `review-payment-status` reads "Az eredmény még nem ismert, ezért nem tekintjük elutasítottnak.", `review-payment-polling` reads "Az állapotot másodpercenként automatikusan ellenőrizzük." and `review-payment-check` is rendered with its inner button "Állapot ellenőrzése". `review-payment-poll-exhausted` is not on screen yet.
11. Wait for the poll to stop on success: the mock's third read of the still-pending intent within this page load resolves it as `succeeded` and persists, and the storefront clears `review-payment-polling` and `review-payment-check` and renders the success branch. - expect: The recorder captures exactly three `GET /api/v1/payment-intents/<id>` requests from this page load (one cold-start read that returns pending, two poll reads — the third read returning succeeded, after which no further polls are scheduled). No `POST /api/v1/payment-intents` and no `POST …/api/v1/orders` was issued without a press. `review-payment-status` reads "A fizetés jóváhagyva.", `review-payment-polling` and `review-payment-poll-exhausted` are not on screen, `review-payment-check` is not on screen. The URL after the poll stopped is still `/penztar/ellenorzes`.
12. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` (a no-op if the round trip preserved a tick), then press the inner button of `review-submit` ("Demórendelés véglegesítése"). - expect: The recorder captures exactly one new `POST /api/v1/orders` returning 201. The browser lands on `/penztar/siker/<orderNumber>`, `order-receipt` is visible and `receipt-order-number` is non-empty. `receipt-row-payment` contains "Bankkártya" and `receipt-row-status` contains "Demó rendelés visszaigazolva".
