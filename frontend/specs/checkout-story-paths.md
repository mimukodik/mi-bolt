# MiBolt checkout story paths test plan

## Application Overview

MiBolt is a Hungarian demo storefront served from the Playwright base URL with the in-page MSW mock behind it: there is no backend and no database, and every product, price, order and payment is demo data. The checkout wizard is the cart `/penztar/kosar`, the contact step `/penztar/adatok`, the delivery step `/penztar/szallitas`, the review step `/penztar/ellenorzes` and the receipt `/penztar/siker/<orderId>`; a card payment leaves the SPA for the demo payment application under `/demo-fizetes/<paymentIntentId>` and returns through the cold-start page at `/penztar?paymentIntentId=<id>`. The mock keeps its state (cart, checkout draft, payment intents, receipts) in the browsing context's `sessionStorage`, and the demo access gate starts signed in, so one fresh browser context IS an empty cart with no sign-in step: every scenario here is independent and can run in any order.

This plan carries the four stories the standard's own rows ask for and no others: the double-clicked final confirmation, the contact field boundaries one at a time, the pickup point, and the change from home delivery to pickup with a card payment already started. Its routes through screens already driven elsewhere are written as the shortest way to reach them, and no assertion of `specs/storefront-integration.md` (its scenarios 3.4 to 3.9), `specs/checkout-edge-paths.md` or `specs/checkout-fault-paths.md` is repeated.

Every number here names its source. The mock's checkout store (`projects/shop-mocks/src/checkout/checkout-store.ts`) sets home delivery "Házhoz szállítás" at 1490 Ft, pickup "Átvételi pont" at 990 Ft and free shipping from a 30 000 Ft subtotal (`DELIVERY_OPTIONS`, `FREE_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD_HUF`), a card payment at 0 Ft and cash on delivery "Utánvét" at 490 Ft (`PAYMENT_METHODS`); the storefront's contact schema (`contact.schema.ts`) sets the name bounds at 2 to 100 characters (`CONTACT_NAME_MIN`, `CONTACT_NAME_MAX`), the e-mail at up to 254 characters behind its RFC 5322 subset pattern (`CONTACT_EMAIL_MAX`, `EMAIL_RFC5322_SUBSET`) and the phone at "+36" plus 8 or 9 national digits (`PHONE_INTERNATIONAL`, `CONTACT_PHONE_NATIONAL_DIGITS_MIN`, `CONTACT_PHONE_NATIONAL_DIGITS_MAX`); every other amount is one the screen showed in the run this plan was written from.

Two rows cannot be tested whole in this build, and both for the same reason. The cheapest phone in the catalogue is the 69 990 Ft "Honor X7D" (measured by sorting the catalogue by price ascending), so every cart the screens can build is above the free-shipping threshold and both delivery options cost 0 Ft there. E078's row asks for "Choose pickup point" → "Shipping 990 Ft below a 30000 Ft subtotal, 0 from it; address omitted, no real location claim."; only its "0 from it" half is reachable, because the 990 Ft needs a cart below the 30 000 Ft subtotal, which needs a fixture this build does not offer. Scenario 1.3 therefore asserts the 0 Ft half together with the omitted address and the "no real location claim" notice, on the delivery step, the review step and the receipt. E081's row asks for "Change home to pickup and return review" → "Recomputed total differs by the fee difference (500 Ft below a 30000 Ft subtotal, none from it); a card payment intent for the old total is refused with 409 PRICE_CHANGED."; its "none from it" half is reachable and is what scenario 1.4 asserts — the total does not move at all when home delivery becomes pickup. The "500 Ft" half and the 409 PRICE_CHANGED refusal both need a cart below the 30 000 Ft subtotal, since only below it do the two fees differ, and that fixture this build does not offer; scenario 1.4 shows what the review page does instead with a card intent started before the change, which is to keep it, because the total it paid for never changed.

E075's row asks for "Use too-short/too-long name,invalid email,wrong phone" → "Each relevant boundary has distinct assertion; no request until valid.", and all four cases are on the screen. One boundary is worth naming for the generator: `contact-fullname-input` carries `maxlength="100"` and `contact-email-input` `maxlength="254"`, so a typed value cannot pass the ceiling; the too-long name is reached by setting a 101-character value past that attribute, which the form's own 100-character rule then reports.

Locator conventions this plan relies on: controls built on the `app-button` primitive are host elements carrying the test id, so a press targets the native button inside them, `page.getByTestId('<id>').getByRole('button')`; prices come from the `app-price-block` primitive, whose digits sit in `[data-testid="price-amount"]` with the "Ft" glyph as a separate span beside them; `page.reload()` is banned by `e2e/seed.spec.ts`, so a reopen is `page.goto(page.url())`. The delivery step renders the delivery address form in an `address-form` element and the billing one in a `billing-address-form` host whose own root carries `address-form` again, so the two share their inner test ids (`address-input-postalCode`, `address-input-city`, `address-input-line1`, `address-input-line2`) and each field is read inside its own container. The two forms add up to one address form on the step when the "same as delivery" tick is on or when pickup is chosen, and to two when the tick is off. On the product page the call to action has two mounts: `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider, and the sticky rail's `purchase-cta` below it; a step that adds a variant names both, and the mobile pass measured the other mount at zero size. Every other checkout control — the contact inputs and `contact-submit`, the delivery radios and `delivery-confirm`, the payment radios, the review cards, `review-edit-delivery`, `review-consent-terms-input`, `review-submit` and the receipt — is present and visible at both widths, measured at 1920x1080 and at 390x844.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Checkout: standard stories

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. [E063] a double-clicked confirmation places exactly one order

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-story-double-click-submit.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/telefonok`, wait for `catalog-grid`, and open the first `product-card` that carries no `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`. - expect: `product-page` is visible, `product-stock` reads "Raktáron a demó készletben", the "Készülékár" `price-amount` reads 89 990, and `cart-badge` in the header reads "0", because a fresh context holds an empty cart.
2. Add the variant to the cart: at 1024 px and wider press the inner button of `add-to-cart-button`; at 390x844 press the inner button of the sticky rail's `purchase-cta`, which is the only mount with a size at that width. - expect: The confirmation message ends with "1 darab a kosárban." and `cart-badge` reads "1".
3. Open the cart with the header's `cart-link` and press the inner button of `cart-continue` ("Tovább az adatokhoz"). - expect: `cart-page` is visible with exactly one `cart-line`. - expect: The URL is `/penztar/adatok` and `contact-page` is visible with the `h1` "Kapcsolattartási adatok".
4. Type "Teszt Elek" into `contact-fullname-input`, "teszt@example.test" into `contact-email-input` and "+36 30 123 4567" into `contact-phone-input`, then press the inner button of `contact-submit` ("Tovább a szállításhoz"). - expect: The URL is `/penztar/szallitas` and `delivery-step` is visible with the `h1` "Átvétel és fizetés".
5. Choose "Házhoz szállítás" by its `delivery-radio`, fill "1052" into `address-input-postalCode`, "Budapest" into `address-input-city` and "Fő utca 1." into `address-input-line1` inside `address-form`, keep the "A számlázási cím megegyezik a szállítási címmel" tick, choose "Utánvét" by its `payment-radio`, then press the inner button of `delivery-confirm` ("Tovább az ellenőrzéshez"). - expect: `summary-row-delivery` names "Házhoz szállítás" and `summary-row-fee` shows "0 Ft": the mock charges no delivery fee from a 30 000 Ft subtotal, and this cart's subtotal is 89 990 Ft. - expect: The URL is `/penztar/ellenorzes` and `review-page` is visible.
6. Tick `review-consent-terms-input` ("Elfogadom a demó rendelési feltételeit.") and start recording the browser's request events: every request whose method is `POST` and whose URL ends in `/api/v1/orders`. - expect: `review-payment` reads "Utánvét" and `review-summary`'s order summary reads Részösszeg 89 990 Ft, Szállítási díj 0 Ft, Fizetési díj 490 Ft and Végösszeg 90 480 Ft. - expect: The inner button of `review-submit` ("Demórendelés véglegesítése") is enabled, no `review-cta-explanation` is rendered, and no order request has been sent yet.
7. Double-click the inner button of `review-submit`. - expect: The browser lands on `/penztar/siker/<orderId>` with an order id in the path: the submit navigates, it does not reload the page. - expect: Exactly one recorded request has the method `POST` and a URL ending in `/api/v1/orders`, and it answers 201. - expect: The double click delivers two click events and both arrive with the button still enabled: the disabled binding follows the submitting state only on the next render, so the button is NOT disabled after the first click (measured: two click events, both with `disabled: false`). The single request comes from the submission's in-flight guard, which hands the second press the first press's promise instead of starting a second order.
8. Read the receipt. - expect: `success-page` and `order-receipt` are each rendered exactly once, `success-heading` reads "Elkészült a demórendelésed." and `success-check` is rendered. - expect: Exactly one `receipt-order-number` is on the page and it is not empty: one receipt opened, and the double press placed no second order. - expect: `receipt-row-payment` contains "Utánvét", `receipt-row-delivery` names "Házhoz szállítás" and `receipt-row-status` reads "Demó rendelés visszaigazolva".

#### 1.2. [E075] the contact step reports each boundary on its own and sends nothing until the form is valid

**File:** `e2e/integration/checkout-story-contact-boundaries.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Reach the contact step: open `/telefonok`, wait for `catalog-grid`, open the first `product-card` without a `product-card-stock` marker through its `product-card-cta`, add the variant with the inner button of `add-to-cart-button` at 1024 px and wider or of the sticky rail's `purchase-cta` at 390x844, open the cart with `cart-link`, and press the inner button of `cart-continue`. Start recording the browser's request events. - expect: `contact-page` is visible with the `h1` "Kapcsolattartási adatok" on `/penztar/adatok`. - expect: `contact-form` shows the hint "A csillaggal jelölt mezők kitöltése kötelező." and `contact-demo-notice` is rendered. - expect: The inner button of `contact-submit` ("Tovább a szállításhoz") is enabled although every field is empty: the step never disables it for an incomplete form, it reports the errors on press.
2. Type "A" into `contact-fullname-input` (one character, below the storefront's floor of 2, `CONTACT_NAME_MIN`), "teszt@example.test" into `contact-email-input` and "+36 30 123 4567" into `contact-phone-input`, then press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: The URL stays `/penztar/adatok` and `contact-page` stays visible. - expect: `contact-summary` appears with the title "Kérlek, javítsd a következő mezőket a folytatás előtt:" and exactly one line reading "Teljes név: Add meg a teljes neved (legalább 2 karakter).". - expect: The message under `contact-fullname-input` reads "Add meg a teljes neved (legalább 2 karakter)." and the input carries `aria-invalid="true"`. - expect: No `PATCH` request to `/api/v1/checkout` has been recorded: the step saves nothing while the form is invalid.
3. Set `contact-fullname-input` to a 101-character name (above the ceiling of 100, `CONTACT_NAME_MAX`; the input carries `maxlength="100"`, so the value is set past that attribute) and press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: `contact-summary` now lists exactly "Teljes név: A teljes név legfeljebb 100 karakter lehet." and the message under `contact-fullname-input` reads the same sentence. - expect: The URL stays `/penztar/adatok` and still no `PATCH` request to `/api/v1/checkout` has been recorded.
4. Set `contact-fullname-input` to "Teszt Elek" and `contact-email-input` to "teszt@" (an address with no valid form: the storefront accepts the RFC 5322 subset its own pattern matches, up to 254 characters), then press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: `contact-summary` lists exactly "E-mail cím: Adj meg egy érvényes e-mail címet." and the message under `contact-email-input` reads the same sentence. - expect: `contact-email-input` carries `aria-invalid="true"`. - expect: The URL stays `/penztar/adatok` and no `PATCH` request to `/api/v1/checkout` has been recorded.
5. Set `contact-email-input` to "teszt@example.test" and `contact-phone-input` to "1234" (a form the storefront refuses: a Hungarian number is "+36" followed by 8 or 9 national digits, with spaces, hyphens, parentheses and a leading national "0" normalized away), then press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: `contact-summary` lists exactly "Telefonszám: Adj meg érvényes magyar (+36) telefonszámot 8-9 jeggyel." and the message under `contact-phone-input` reads the same sentence. - expect: `contact-phone-input` carries `aria-invalid="true"`. - expect: The URL stays `/penztar/adatok` and no `PATCH` request to `/api/v1/checkout` has been recorded.
6. Correct the phone to "+36 30 123 4567" and press the inner button of `contact-submit`. - expect: Exactly one `PATCH` request to `/api/v1/checkout` has been recorded and it answers 200: the step saves only once the form is locally valid. - expect: `contact-summary` is gone and the wizard advances: the URL is `/penztar/szallitas` and `delivery-step` is visible with the `h1` "Átvétel és fizetés".

#### 1.3. [E078] the pickup point ships free, asks for no delivery address and claims no real location

#### 1.4. [E081] changing home delivery to pickup keeps the total and keeps the started card payment
