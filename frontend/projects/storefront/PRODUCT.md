# MiBolt storefront

## Register

brand

## Users

Hungarian shoppers compare fictional phones, often on small mobile screens, and rehearse a simulated purchase.

## Product Purpose

A demo webshop with catalogue, variant selection, cart and simulated checkout. BOLT-6 adds reusable promotions and a Halloween campaign.

## Brand Personality

Playful, bold and clear. Preserve the existing MiBolt navy/lime identity and typography; the approved Halloween campaign adds a purple/orange night scene.

## Anti-references

No decoration over prices, currency, names or controls. No animated catalogue swaps, replacement typography or additional commerce services.

## Design Principles

Show the concrete selected variant price. Keep featured selection independent of promotions. Make mobile shopping immediately usable. Preserve the existing page geometry. Make campaign editing reusable from the admin.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve native controls and existing semantics. Reduced motion presents all artwork immediately and stops decorative motion. Verify desktop and 320/390 px mobile layouts with long names and large amounts.

## Sources

Repository frontend specification and design reference; BOLT-6 and its approved implementation plan.
