# MiBolt product context

MiBolt is a Hungarian demonstration webshop for phones, with a separate back-office
for administrators. Shoppers compare selected colour/storage variants and complete
a demonstrably safe purchase flow. Administrators maintain catalogue and campaign
data without developer assistance.

Preserve the existing layout, typography, MiBolt logo and navigation. The approved
Halloween direction is B / Boszorkány: purple and orange, visibly festive but legible
on desktop and mobile. Spider, bats, moon and jack-o'-lantern are decorative only:
they must not cover controls, text or prices, intercept clicks or enter accessibility
names. Do not introduce flashing or flying decorations.

Use server-authoritative prices and campaign status; never invent discounts. Show
the current regular price crossed out and an explicit sale price for in-stock sale
variants. Keep amounts and Ft together. Preserve the four featured-phone selection.
The banner copy is exactly “Válassz ijesztően jó halloweeni ajánlataink közül!”.

The execution specification and accepted business decisions live in BOLT-5 and its
linked Plan; those take precedence over earlier proposals or illustrative prices.
